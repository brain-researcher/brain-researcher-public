Brain Researcher Helm rendering notice

This chart is an experimental static-rendering scaffold. It is not a supported
installation or production deployment target. Template rendering does not prove
that workloads are runnable, healthy, or safe to expose.

The application image references use fixed component preview tags for
deterministic inspection. `global.imageTag`, when set, overrides structured
component `image.tag` values; deprecated component `imageTag` compatibility
fields retain higher precedence. Brain Researcher does not publish or support
a production image contract for this chart. Operators evaluating the output
must supply and verify their own images.

This chart emits no Secret resources. The rendered workloads refer to
pre-created Secrets declared under values.yaml `secrets` and `neo4j.auth`:
