\section{\texorpdfstring{\textbf{Appendix E. Constraint compiler, pre-execution validity checks, and commitment-gate card}}{Appendix E. Constraint compiler, pre-execution validity checks, and commitment-gate card}}\label{appendix-e.-constraint-compiler-pre-execution-validity-checks-and-commitment-gate-card}\label{app:e}

\hyperref[app:e]{Appendix E} is the pre-execution validity gate: it proves which hard and soft constraints were active, how they were bound to the selected plan, and who allowed, revised, or blocked execution.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{\textbf{E1. Purpose and scope}}{E1. Purpose and scope}}\label{e1.-purpose-and-scope}

This card records the active validity boundary between planning and execution. It documents the hard and soft constraints compiled for the selected workflow, their provenance, bound episode fields, pass/\allowbreak{}warn/\allowbreak{}block outcomes, required sensitivity analyses, caveat requirements, and commitment-gate decision.
